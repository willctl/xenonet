An intent is work you ask for at one of your machines: a goal, the
project it's in and the machine to do it, or any machine your delegation
names. That machine checks it against your delegation, starts a session
in a checkout of the project you confirmed there, and reports back:
started, notes on the way, and what came of it.

  xenonet intent list [--json]     your intents and how each stands
  xenonet intent make "<goal>" --on <machine>|any --project <prj_…>
      [--app claude|codex|grok] [--delegation <dlg_…>] [--hours <n>]
      [--arbiter <machine>]
  xenonet intent cancel <int_…>    stops it, and its session

Making and cancelling an intent sign as you, so they're done in
XenoNet's window (Intents, in the command palette), where your device
signs them. From a terminal they need your presence, which XenoNet asks
for only in its window, so here they're refused and say so.

An intent runs under the one delegation of yours it names. Leave it out
and XenoNet picks one that names both machines for starts. Revoking that
delegation stops what wasn't started yet. An intent lasts a day unless
you say otherwise (--hours), a month at most.

With --on any, each machine your delegation names that has a checkout of
the project you confirmed in its window offers to take it, and the
arbiter gives it to the first: this machine, or --arbiter, a machine
your delegation names, for when this one may be asleep. It's given once.
Until the arbiter gives it out it waits, and the list says on whom.
