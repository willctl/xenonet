XenoNet is installed

XenoNet is open now. It lives in the menu bar: closing its window keeps it running, and Quit XenoNet is in the menu bar icon's menu.

The first time it opens, XenoNet asks whether to open at login. You can change that later from the menu bar icon.

To uninstall, choose XenoNet › Uninstall XenoNet… It asks whether to remove your data too.
