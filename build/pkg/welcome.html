Set up XenoNet

XenoNet is a live console for your projects and the Claude Code and Codex sessions working in them. It shows what's running, tells you when a session needs you or two sessions edit the same file, and lets you open, hand off or inspect any of it from one place.

On the next page, choose who it's for: everyone on this Mac, in Applications, which asks for an administrator password, or just you, in your home folder's Applications, which doesn't.

If XenoNet is running, the installer asks to close it first.
